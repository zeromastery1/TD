# 3D model credits

The compiled models in `data/` were made by the Emberfall Survivors model
pipeline (`zeromastery1/SurvivorGame`, `tools/models`), which repaints, resizes
and thins public-domain models and bakes their animation into bone tables.
Every source model is Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). CC0 does not require
credit; the artists are credited here anyway.

## Quaternius — https://quaternius.com

Animated monsters and props, CC0 1.0.

- Creeps: `zombie`, `wolf`, `orc_brute`, `crab`, `slime_green`, `bat`
- Bosses: `yeti`, `wyvern`, `demon`

## Kenney — https://www.kenney.nl

From the Nature Kit, CC0 1.0.

- Trees: `tree_oak`, `tree_dark`, `tree_fat`
- Rocks and plants: `rock_large`, `rock_tall`, `rock_basalt`, `bush`, `stump`, `log`
- Stonework: `obelisk`, `column`, `column_broken`, `campfire`
